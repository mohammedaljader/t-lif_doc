## Scope and status

- “T-LIF DOC” is the requested website name. It does not rename the underlying T-LIF system.
- Source documents describe plans and prototypes at their respective dates. The site does not claim specific capabilities are deployed or that planned evaluations are complete.
- Evaluation aims are distinguished from proven results. No outcomes, participant counts, awards or release dates are invented.
- Teacher review is retained because the September workflow slides specify it and the public project page emphasizes teacher control. The approval-removal suggestion is not presented as a decision.
- Canvas, Athena, Osiris and YUJA are documented source systems, not integrations implemented by this website.
- The demo section now links to https://teacher.t-lif.com/ and https://student.t-lif.com/, as supplied by the user. The documentation app contains no local AI or feedback simulation.
- Research descriptions paraphrase source material. The referenced paper is presented as a cited foundation, not as a new T-LIF publication.
- The original PolyFeed reference informs page organization only. Its institutional claims, people, awards and assets are not used.
